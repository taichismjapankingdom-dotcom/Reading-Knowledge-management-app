# Photosynthesis Overview

Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants and some other organisms use sunlight to synthesize foods from carbon dioxide and water.

## Two Main Stages

1.  **Light-dependent reactions:**
    *   Occur in the thylakoid membrane.
    *   Require continuous sunlight.
    *   Produce ATP and NADPH.
    *   Release oxygen as a byproduct from the splitting of water.

2.  **Calvin cycle (Light-independent reactions):**
    *   Takes place in the stroma.
    *   Does not require light directly.
    *   Uses ATP and NADPH to convert CO2 into glucose.

### Key Components

*   **Chloroplasts:** The organelles where photosynthesis occurs.
*   **Chlorophyll:** The green pigment that captures light energy.
*   **Stomata:** Pores on the leaf surface that allow gas exchange (CO2 in, O2 out).
